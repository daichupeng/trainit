# Training History

## Recording Rules & Provenance
- Include only completed activity or clearly labeled historical tests/results supplied by this user. Never log an interview, prescription, example, or future arrangement as completed training.
- Use `## YYYY-MM-DD | Session ID | Activity Type`. IDs must be unique; suggested form `B01-YYYYMMDD-S01` or `HIST-001`. Use `Date unknown` explicitly when necessary. Deduplicate by date, ID, and content.
- Insert new completed sessions after these rules and before existing sessions. Preserve history and date/reason/source for additions or corrections; do not silently replace original facts.
- For strength, retain every reported set's exercise, load/units, repetitions, warm-up/work designation, and actual reported RIR/RPE. Mark unknown types/values honestly. Exercise-wide RIR remains exercise-wide; a target is not an observed RIR.
- For other activities, preserve appropriate reported duration, moving/elapsed time, distance, pace/speed, heart rate, ascent/descent, altitude, terrain, load, laps, and experience. Omit irrelevant fields. Treat suitable device measurements as reported; do not assume a particular sensor.
- Reuse conventions confirmed in profile.md and explicit session overrides. Ask about material ambiguity (per dumbbell/total, per side/bilateral, bar included, actual/target or per-set/overall RIR, moving/elapsed time). Do not infer missing conventions.
- Ask whether important missing records/feedback exist; until answered, use pending. If unavailable, agree an accurate label (not measured, not recorded, cannot recall) and do not keep asking. Nonessential fields do not block recording known facts or normal training.
- Check unresolved prior recovery at natural conversation, separating current state from earlier aftereffects. Unobserved future recovery remains pending. Record verification is separate from eligibility to train.
- Add later recovery to the original Session under `### Recovery Follow-up — YYYY-MM-DD` with supplementation date, period/source, and limits; never create a new session or attribute it to the receipt-date workout.
- For inseparable recovery from consecutive sessions, identify all related IDs, keep full feedback under the most recent related session, and cross-reference the others. Preserve original dates/wording and traceable correction reasons.
- Separate user reports, device outputs, calculations, coaching judgments, and unknowns. Keep dates in the user's confirmed time zone and original units with labeled approximate conversions.
