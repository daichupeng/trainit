# Current Training State

## Maintenance Conventions
- Follow the root `capability_assessment_methodology.md`. Lead with current capacity/limitations, then evidence and its limits, then training implications/next verification.
- Separate observed performance, model estimates, coaching judgment, and unknowns. Do not turn interview confidence into measured capacity.
- Keep original observation dates and Session IDs; raw completed facts/corrections stay in training_log.md and full prescriptions in training_plan.md.
- Update only changed capacity, evidence, recent load, recovery, and execution progress. Do not accumulate log-like summaries.
