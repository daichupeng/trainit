# Current Training Plan

## Maintenance Conventions

- This is a template, not an existing training plan. Instantiate at the project root only after the interview is sufficient to produce a usable first plan; remove all placeholders/comments and unused activity examples.
- Keep this independent section immediately after the title. The four body sections are Block Information, Training Overview, Session Guidelines, and Current Progress.
- Session Guidelines is the sole active-prescription source. Replace parameters/progression status in place; do not append adjustment histories, daily changes, obsolete prescriptions, or plan copies.
- Completed facts/corrections stay in training_log.md; capacity, recent load, recovery and execution metadata stay in current_status.md. Current Progress is a concise execution summary.
- Routine parameter changes do not upgrade the block version. Review strategy at block end, explicit request, or a major change; keep only brief prior-block conclusions.
